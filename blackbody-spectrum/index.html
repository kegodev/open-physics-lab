What this simulator demonstrates

A blackbody is an ideal object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation and emits a continuous spectrum determined only by its temperature. Increasing temperature raises the emitted intensity dramatically and shifts the peak toward shorter wavelengths.

B(λ,T) = (2hc² / λ⁵) · 1 / (e^(hc/λkT) − 1)

Visible-spectrum reference

At 5778 K, the blackbody peak lies near the visible region.
